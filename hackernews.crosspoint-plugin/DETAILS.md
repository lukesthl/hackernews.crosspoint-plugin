# Hacker News

Browse and search Hacker News on your reader, and save the linked articles as plain text to read offline.

- **Lists:** Front Page, Newest and Show HN, plus search.
- **Save an article:** select a story and its article is saved to `/Hacker News` as a `.txt` file.
- **No setup:** no account, no API key, no companion app.

## Limits

- PDFs, videos and JavaScript-only sites do not convert to readable text.
- Stories without a link (most Ask HN posts) cannot be saved.
- Comments are not included.

## Privacy

Story lists come from the Algolia Hacker News API. Article text comes from the Jina Reader service (`r.jina.ai`), which receives the address of each article you save.
