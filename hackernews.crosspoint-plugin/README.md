Hacker News

Browse and search Hacker News on the reader and save linked
articles as plain text for offline reading.

How to use
1. Connect the reader to Wi-Fi.
2. Open Home > Plugins > Hacker News.
3. Pick a list: Front Page, Newest or Show HN. Search is also
   available.
4. Select a story to save its article. It lands in the
   "Hacker News" folder on the SD card as a .txt file.

Good to know
- No account or setup is needed.
- Stories come from the Algolia Hacker News API.
- Articles are converted to text by the Jina Reader service
  (r.jina.ai). The article address is sent to that service.
- Some links cannot be converted: PDFs, videos and sites that
  need JavaScript give an empty or very short file.
- Stories without a link (most Ask HN posts) cannot be saved.
- Comments are not included.
